# Improving the trans-ancestry portability of polygenic risk scores by prioritizing variants in predicted cell-type-specific regulatory elements
**https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-020-00740-8** 
**Read:** July 24, 2026

## Main Ideas
- Trans-ancestry portability becomes an issue when GWAS uses Eurocentric datasets: tagging variants (LD-tagging SNPs) have poor trans-ancestry portability because LD-relationships with causal variants differ by ancestry.
- IMPACT creates weights (variant prioritization scores) based on epigenomic and sequence features to emphasize variants that are likely functional rather than merely LD associated. 
- IMPACT-prioritized variants show greater cross-ancestry concordance in GWAS marginal effect sizes
- With IMPACT-prioritized variants there is 49.9% mean relative increase (to PRS baseline) in $ R^2 $, the phenotype variance explained by European-trained polygenic risk scores (PRS) when evaluated in East Asian populations.
